REMEMBERING STEVE JOBS

Steve Jobs (1955–2011) was an American entrepreneur, inventor, and co-founder of Apple Inc. He was known for revolutionizing personal computing, smartphones, and digital animation

TIMELINE AND ACHIEVEMENTS

  • 1976 – Co-founded Apple in his garage.
  • 1984 – Launched the first Macintosh.
  • 1985 – Left Apple.
  • 1997 – Returned to Apple, saving it from bankruptcy.
  • 2001 – Released the first iPod.
  • 2007 – Introduced the iPhone.
  • 2011 – Passed away, leaving a lasting legacy.
